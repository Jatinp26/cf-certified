Why build on the Canton Network?

The Canton Network is a public, permissionless blockchain built for regulated finance. It combines institutional-grade privacy and control with the composability and open access of a public network.

Real assets at scale

More than $6 trillion in on-chain assets and over $280 billion in daily
U.S. Treasury repo already run on Canton Network applications.

Privacy at the protocol level

Each party sees only the data it is entitled to, while the network guarantees a shared, consistent outcome.

Composable across the network

Through the Global Synchronizer, applications operated by different organizations settle atomically in a single transaction.

Rewards for builders

Network economics reward the application providers and validators that generate real activity.

What is Daml?

Daml is the smart contract language used to build applications on the Canton Network. Where Solidity serves the EVM, Daml is purpose-built for the network, designed around two questions most languages leave to the developer: who is authorized to act, and who is entitled to see the data.

  • Signatories

    Must authorize a contract before it exists, so nobody can put obligations on you without your consent.

  • Observers

    Can see a contract without being bound by it.

  • Controllers

    Define exactly who can exercise each choice.

Daml 3 runs on Canton 3.x, the software that powers the Canton Network today. It is the recommended foundation for all new projects.

What does certification demonstrate?

Learning Daml requires no certificate. Passing an exam shows that you can apply the skills each path teaches.

  • Authorization and privacy

    Applying Daml's model of who can act on a contract and who can see it.

  • Contract development and testing

    Writing Daml 3 templates and choices, and testing them with Daml Script.

  • Application integration

    Connecting contracts to backends and frontends through the Ledger API (Path B).

  • Working on the current stack

    Building with Daml 3 and Canton 3.x, the software behind the Canton Network today.

  • Issued by Canton Foundation

    Each credential is issued by Canton Foundation.

Frequently asked questions

Fees, eligibility and support for the Canton Developer Certification.

What are the course and certification fees?
Courses are free. Each certification exam costs US$150.
Can I get an exam fee waiver?
Yes, in certain cases. Canton Foundation issues coupon codes that waive the exam fee, distributed through ecosystem programs, events and partner teams.
Is the program suitable for Ethereum and Solidity developers?
Yes. Daml 3 Foundations requires no prior experience with the Canton Network. Concepts such as contracts, tokens and transactions will be familiar; however, network's privacy and authorization model differs significantly, and the course considerably shortens the learning curve.
Are Daml 2 courses still offered?
Yes. Our certification program is centered on Daml 3 to align with the latest Canton updates and best practices. Daml 2 resources remain available solely to support existing applications.
What support is available during the program?
Participants can raise questions in the Canton Developer Forum and consult the official documentation at docs.canton.network.
