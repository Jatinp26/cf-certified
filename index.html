What Is Daml?

Daml is the smart contract language of Canton. Where Solidity serves the EVM, Daml is purpose-built for Canton, designed around two questions most languages leave to the developer: who is authorized to act, and who is entitled to see the data.

  • Signatories

    Must authorize a contract before it exists, so nobody can put obligations on you without your consent.

  • Observers

    Can see a contract without being bound by it.

  • Controllers

    Define exactly who can exercise each choice.

Daml 3 runs on Canton 3.x, the stack that powers today's Canton Network. It is the recommended foundation for all new projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fees, eligibility and support for the Canton Developer Certification.

What are the course and certification fees?
All courses are offered free of charge. Each certification exam carries a fee of $150.
Are exam fee waivers available?
Yes, in certain cases. Canton Foundation issues coupon codes that waive the exam fee, distributed through ecosystem programs, events and partner teams.
Is the program suitable for Ethereum and Solidity developers?
Yes. Daml 3 Foundations requires no prior Canton experience. Concepts such as contracts, tokens and transactions will be familiar; however, Canton's privacy and authorization model differs significantly, and the course considerably shortens the learning curve.
Are Daml 2 courses still offered?
Yes. Daml 2 courses remain available to support institutions still operating on Daml 2; however, certification is actively promoted only for the Daml 3 track.
What support is available during the program?
Participants can raise questions in the Canton Developer Forum and consult the official documentation at docs.canton.network.
